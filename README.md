## The problem
Solar tricycles (Kekes) are running on the University of Benin campus, but there is no digital system behind them. Drivers cruise aimlessly for riders and burn charge. Payments are made through long steps in [revcollect website](https://uniben.revcollect.com/) , which means slow boarding. Nobody can see battery status remotely, so vehicles strand mid-route.

## What USRide does
- **Live geolocation:** real-time Keke positions and rider booking
- **Eco-routing:** groups riders heading the same way to save battery
- **Instant cashless wallet:** top-up and payouts via Paystack
- **Battery and trip tracking:** drivers see trips remaining per charge
- **SOS alert:** emergency alarm built into every ride
- **Analytics dashboard:** rides, revenue, earnings and logs
- **Driver daily earnings tracker**

## Who it's for
- **Students and lecturers** who want fast, cashless campus rides
- **Drivers** who want steady income, fewer idle kilometres and a verifiable earnings record
- **Campus transport operators** who need visibility over their fleet

## Roadmap
- Live driver navigation to the rider
- Battery life prediction and analytics engine
- Carbon emissions saved tracking
- Driver income score for micro-loans and asset financing
- USSD and offline access for low-connectivity users
